Copium Videos

Short, fact-checked gaming news with an opinion. On the side of players.

Copium Videos is a YouTube Shorts channel. Each Short covers one gaming news story in under a minute: the facts first, checked against several outlets, then a clear take, then a question for the comments.

Copium Uploader

Copium Uploader is the channel's own publishing tool. It runs on the channel owner's computer and uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload the channel's Shorts and read how they perform. It only ever signs in to the channel's own Google account and is not offered to anyone else.
